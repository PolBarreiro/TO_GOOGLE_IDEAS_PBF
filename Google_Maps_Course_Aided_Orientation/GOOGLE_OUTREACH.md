# Draft licensing inquiry

**Subject:** Commercial licensing inquiry: GPS-course aid for Google Maps direction indicator

Hello Google Maps team,

I am sharing a small concept and Python demonstration for stabilizing the forward-direction indicator in map applications.

The proposal uses the recent direction of travel from successive GPS fixes as an additional heading cue, under the assumption that the user holds the phone upright and points it forward. A production implementation could fuse GPS course with existing device-orientation sensors and confidence estimates. The goal is to improve the map's displayed orientation when heading is noisy; it does not claim to improve the GPS position itself.

The concept, limitations, and demonstration are here:
https://github.com/PolBarreiro/TO_GOOGLE_IDEAS_PBF/tree/main/Google_Maps_Course_Aided_Orientation

I am open to an economical commercial license or buyout, with scope and price to be agreed in writing. The public materials are offered for non-commercial sharing with attribution and without derivatives under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Please let me know whether Google has an appropriate channel for a confidential licensing discussion before I submit this through any product-feedback process.
